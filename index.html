📐 Setup & Positioning Guide

1

Place Phone on the Floor

Prop your phone up against a wall or bottle on the floor, roughly 2 to 3 meters (7-10 feet) away.

2

Side Profile View

Position yourself completely side-on to the camera. The app automatically detects which side faces the camera (left or right).

3

Full Body in Frame

Ensure your head, shoulders, hips, and ankles remain visible throughout both the top and bottom of the push-up.

4

Good Contrast & Lighting

Avoid harsh backlights (like pointing directly into a bright window). Wear clothes contrasting with your floor background.
